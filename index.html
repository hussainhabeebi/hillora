Where is Hillora Tales located?

Hillora Tales is a resort in Vagamon, a hill station in the Idukki district of Kerala, India. Vagamon is known for its pine forests, rolling meadows and tea gardens.

Does the resort have a swimming pool?

Yes. Every stay at Hillora Tales includes access to the swimming pool, which is open from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Staff can extend the hours on request.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, complimentary breakfast is included with your stay. Dinner is available at the restaurant at extra cost unless your package includes it.

What types of stays are available?

There are four private stays: Amrutham Villa and Aranyam Villa (independent 3-bedroom villas), Cleopatra (an A-frame cottage with a glass balcony) and The Pool Deck (a 2-bedroom apartment with pool and valley views).

Is Hillora Tales good for families and groups?

Yes. The 3-bedroom villas suit families and groups of friends, and the resort has a kids play area, BBQ & campfire evenings and jeep trekking for sightseeing.

What are the check-in time and rates?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM. Rates start from ₹4,500 per night on weekdays and ₹5,200 on weekends and holidays. Our team confirms the final rate for your dates.

How do I book a stay?
